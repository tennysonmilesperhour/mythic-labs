# Expression Markers by Archetype

Starting tendencies, not rules. Every rule in an Expression Spec must still be
justified by the specific brand's structure — these markers only tell you
where to start looking.

| Archetype | Voice register | Rhythm | Stance to reader | Visual temperature & density | Interface metaphor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Innocent | Plain, warm, hopeful | Short, even | Gentle companion | Light, airy, generous white space | Clean slate, fresh start |
| Explorer | Spare, direct, outward-looking | Clipped, forward-moving | Fellow traveler | Natural, earthy, wide frames | Map, trail, horizon |
| Sage | Precise, measured, evidence-led | Long, even, complete | Patient teacher | Cool to neutral, restrained, typographic | Library, index, lens |
| Hero | Active verbs, challenge framing | Punchy, building | Coach who believes in you | High contrast, bold, kinetic | Arena, scoreboard, milestone |
| Outlaw | Defiant, blunt, anti-jargon | Staccato, abrupt | Co-conspirator | Dark, raw, deliberate roughness | Underground, hack, override |
| Magician | Evocative, transformational | Rising, revelatory | Guide to the hidden | Deep, luminous, layered | Portal, reveal, before/after |
| Everyman | Conversational, unpretentious | Natural speech | Neighbor | Familiar, honest, unstyled | Kitchen table, toolbox |
| Lover | Sensory, intimate | Flowing, lingering | Confidant | Warm, rich, tactile close-ups | Invitation, ritual, keepsake |
| Jester | Witty, surprising | Quick, playful | Friend who makes you laugh | Bright, saturated, unexpected | Game, toy, easter egg |
| Caregiver | Reassuring, warm, attentive | Calm, steady | Protector | Soft, warm, rounded | Shelter, hand, safety net |
| Creator | Craft vocabulary, specific | Considered, iterative | Fellow maker | Material, textured, process visible | Studio, workbench, canvas |
| Ruler | Formal, confident, exact | Measured, declarative | Authority | Rich, ordered, symmetrical | Command center, dashboard, seal |

## Translating secondary archetypes

The secondary archetype modulates the dominant — it never overrides it. Use it
for one dimension at most (e.g. a Sage dominant with Jester secondary keeps
Sage voice and structure but allows wit in microcopy and empty states).
