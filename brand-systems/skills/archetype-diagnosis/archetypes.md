# Archetype Reference

Twelve archetypes in four orientations. Use this to shortlist candidates, then
separate close pairs with the discriminating questions at the end.

## Yearning for paradise: independence & fulfillment

| | Innocent | Explorer | Sage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core desire | Safety, goodness, simplicity | Freedom, authentic self-discovery | Truth, understanding |
| Core fear | Doing wrong, being punished | Conformity, entrapment | Deception, ignorance |
| Gift | Faith, optimism | Autonomy, ambition | Wisdom, clarity |
| Shadow form | Denial, naivety | Aimlessness, chronic dissatisfaction | Detachment, condescension |
| Tells in artifacts | Purity language, nostalgia, white space, "simple" | Horizons, solitude, "beyond", rugged materials | Evidence, citations, measured cadence, restraint |

## Leaving a mark: risk & mastery

| | Hero | Outlaw | Magician |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core desire | Prove worth through courageous mastery | Overturn what isn't working | Make transformation happen |
| Core fear | Weakness, vulnerability | Powerlessness, irrelevance | Unintended consequences |
| Gift | Competence, courage | Liberation, radical freedom | Vision, transformation |
| Shadow form | Arrogance, need for enemies | Destruction, nihilism | Manipulation, opacity |
| Tells in artifacts | Challenge framing, metrics, an opponent, "push" | Anti-establishment, rule-breaking, defiance | Before/after, "transform", effortless, mystery |

## Connecting with others: belonging & enjoyment

| | Everyman | Lover | Jester |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core desire | Belonging, connection | Intimacy, experience | Joy in the moment |
| Core fear | Exclusion, standing out | Being alone, unwanted | Boredom, being boring |
| Gift | Realism, empathy, no pretense | Passion, commitment | Lightness, irreverence |
| Shadow form | Self-erasure, blandness | Obsession, people-pleasing | Cruelty as humor, unseriousness |
| Tells in artifacts | Plain talk, everyday scenes, fair price | Sensory language, close-ups, indulgence | Wordplay, surprise, color, self-deprecation |

## Providing structure: stability & control

| | Caregiver | Creator | Ruler |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core desire | Protect and care for others | Create something of enduring value | Order, control, stability |
| Core fear | Selfishness, ingratitude | Mediocre vision or execution | Chaos, being overthrown |
| Gift | Compassion, generosity | Imagination, craft | Responsibility, leadership |
| Shadow form | Martyrdom, smothering | Perfectionism, self-indulgence | Rigidity, authoritarianism |
| Tells in artifacts | Reassurance, "we're here", warmth, softness | Tools, process, "make", visible craft | Premium, exclusivity, symmetry, institutional tone |

## Discriminating questions for commonly confused pairs

| Pair | Ask | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Explorer / Hero | Does it free you *from* something, or make you better *at* something? | From → Explorer. At → Hero. |
| Hero / Outlaw | Does it win inside the rules or reject the rules? | Inside → Hero. Reject → Outlaw. |
| Sage / Magician | Is the mechanism explained or hidden? | Explained → Sage. Hidden, wondrous → Magician. |
| Creator / Magician | Does the user make the result, or is it made for them? | User makes → Creator. Made for them → Magician. |
| Caregiver / Innocent | Does it protect you, or restore your goodness? | Protect → Caregiver. Restore → Innocent. |
| Caregiver / Lover | Is the warmth protective or intimate? | Protective → Caregiver. Intimate, sensory → Lover. |
| Ruler / Sage | Is authority earned by control or by knowledge? | Control → Ruler. Knowledge → Sage. |
| Everyman / Jester | Both unpretentious: does it belong or play? | Belong → Everyman. Play → Jester. |
| Outlaw / Jester | Both irreverent: angry at the system or laughing at it? | Angry → Outlaw. Laughing → Jester. |
| Innocent / Everyman | Aspirational purity or realist belonging? | Purity → Innocent. Realism → Everyman. |

## Evidence weighting

Product **behavior** (what it does, what it charges, what it makes hard) outweighs
**copy** (what it says), which outweighs **stated values** (what it claims to
believe). When behavior and copy disagree, the behavior is the foundation and
the copy is the costume.
