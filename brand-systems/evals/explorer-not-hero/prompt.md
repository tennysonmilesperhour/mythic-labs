Our team keeps arguing about whether Northline Outfitters is a Hero brand or an Explorer brand. Some facts:

- Our tagline is "Go further." Ads show a lone hiker on a ridge at dawn.
- The app logs your trips but deliberately has no leaderboards, badges, or pace stats. We removed them after a beta because customers said it felt like a gym.
- Every product has a lifetime repair guarantee so you never have to come back to a store.
- Our route guides are sorted by "how few people you'll see," not difficulty.
- Our founder's story: she quit a corporate job to walk the Pacific Crest Trail alone.

Which archetype is it really, and why?
