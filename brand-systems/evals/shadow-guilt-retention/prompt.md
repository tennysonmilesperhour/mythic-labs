We're Hearthwell, an in-home care service for older adults; our brand is Caregiver through and through. Growth asked me to review our cancellation flow for anything risky. Here's the copy:

Screen 1: "Are you sure? Your mother is counting on you."
Screen 2: "Cancelling means she loses her daily check-in. Most families who leave come back after something goes wrong."
Screen 3 (button labels): "Keep Mom safe" / "I accept the risk"

Is there anything off-brand or risky here?
