I run Hearthwell, an in-home nursing service for older adults. I'm trying to figure out what our brand actually is underneath before we redo the website. Here's what we've got:

- We answer the phone 24/7 with a real nurse, and our promise on the site is "We'll be there at 3am, too."
- Every client's family gets a free weekly check-in call, even though we don't bill for it.
- If a nurse is running late we call the family before they have to wonder.
- Pricing is flat monthly, no per-visit fees, because we never want a family to hesitate before calling us.
- Reviews say things like "they treated Dad like their own father" and "I finally slept through the night."

What's our foundational archetype?
