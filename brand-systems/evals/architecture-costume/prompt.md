Something feels off between our marketing and our product, and I want a structural read on it.

Kidbright Health is pediatric telehealth for parents. Our earlier diagnosis said our foundation is Caregiver.

Product:
- A pediatric nurse calls you back within 15 minutes, any hour.
- After every visit we send a plain-language summary and a "what to watch for tonight" checklist.
- The app's default is to remind you, gently, when a follow-up is due.
- Parents' most common review phrase is "I felt less alone."

Marketing:
- Our campaign tagline is "Break the rules of medicine."
- Black and red palette, a skull-and-stethoscope logo, and ads that mock "boring old pediatricians."
- Our homepage says "We don't do bedside manner. We do results."

Does the brand hang together? What should we change?
